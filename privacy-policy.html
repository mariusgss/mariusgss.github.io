Privacy Policy

Live Life Free: Learn ETFs · Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: you can use the app without an account, and then your progress stays on your phone only. If you create an account, your name, profile picture and learning progress are stored securely so you can use them on other phones. The app is ad-free: an ad is only shown if you choose to watch one to support the app. We never sell your data.

Who we are

This app is made by Sausis Interactive (“we”, “us”). For any question about your data, contact us at forward-email@sausisinteractive.com.

Using the app without an account

Your progress (lessons completed, quizzes passed, exam score, simulator results and badges) is saved only on your phone. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

If you create an account

You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password. We use Firebase Authentication (by Google) for this, which stores:

  • your email address,
  • your name and, with Google sign-in, your Google profile picture,
  • technical data needed to keep your account secure, such as sign-in times and IP address.

Passwords are handled by Firebase and are never visible to us.

To keep your progress across phones, we store in Cloud Firestore (by Google): your name, the link to your profile picture, and your learning progress (the same items listed above). We do not store your email address there.

Ads (only if you choose to watch one)

The app shows no ads on its own. In your profile you can choose to watch a short ad to support the app. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Why we use your data

  • To run your account and keep your progress (based on our agreement with you when you sign up).
  • To show an ad you asked to watch, and personalised ads only with your consent.
  • To keep the app secure and working.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for anything else.

Where your data is stored

Account data is stored by Google (Firebase) on secure servers in the European Union. Google may process some data in other countries under safeguards required by EU law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Data saved on your phone stays until you uninstall the app.

Deleting your account

Open your profile in the app and tap Delete account. This deletes your account and the progress stored online straight away. You can also ask us to delete it by emailing forward-email@sausisinteractive.com.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Contact us at forward-email@sausisinteractive.com. If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Not financial advice

The app is for learning only. Its simulators use past or made-up data and are not investment advice.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
