Privacy Policy

Live Life Free: Learn ETFs · Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: you can use the app without an account, and then your progress stays on your phone only. If you create an account, your name, profile picture and learning progress are stored securely so you can use them on other phones. The app shows no ads unless you choose to watch one to support it. We never sell your data.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Marius Smalakys, a sole trader based in Malta, trading as Sausis Interactive (“we”, “us”).

Contact for any privacy question or request: forward-email@sausisinteractive.com.

Using the app without an account

Your progress (lessons completed, quizzes passed, exam score, simulator results, badges and how many support ads you watched) is saved only on your phone. It is never sent to us and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

If you create an account

Creating an account is optional. Without one you can still use the whole app; you just can’t keep your progress across phones.

You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password. We use Firebase Authentication (by Google) for this, which stores:

  • your email address and a user ID,
  • your name and, with Google sign-in, your Google profile picture,
  • technical data needed to keep your account secure, such as sign-in times and IP address.

Passwords are handled by Firebase and are never visible to us.

To keep your progress across phones, we store in Cloud Firestore (by Google): your name, the link to your profile picture, your learning progress (the items listed above), and when your account data was created and last updated. We do not store your email address there.

Ads (only if you choose to watch one)

The app shows no ads on its own. You can choose to watch a short ad to support the app. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When you ask for an ad, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud. The app doesn’t request any ad until you first ask for one. After that, it may get the next ad ready in the background (for example when you open your profile) so it plays straight away; it is only shown if you choose to watch it.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the first time you ask for an ad Google shows you a consent message. There you choose whether Google may store and access information on your device, and whether ads may be personalised. If you don’t agree, you may see a non-personalised or limited ad, or no ad at all. Either way you can keep using the whole app.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: tap the support button and then Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Google handles ad data under its own privacy policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Why we use your data, and our legal basis

Under the GDPR we may only use personal data with a legal basis. Ours are:

  • Running your account and syncing your progress, including counting the support ads you watched for your badges: performance of our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), which starts when you create an account.
  • Keeping accounts and the app secure, for example sign-in times and IP addresses to spot misuse: our legitimate interest in protecting the app and its users (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Showing an ad you asked to watch, including storing or reading information on your device for ads: your consent where the law requires it, for example in the EU (Article 6(1)(a)). Elsewhere, our legitimate interest in funding the free app with ads that you choose to watch (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Answering your emails and requests: our legitimate interest in helping you (Article 6(1)(f)), and our legal obligations when you exercise your rights (Article 6(1)(c)).

We do not sell your data or use it for anything else, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who receives your data

Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google Sign-In) processes account data on our behalf as our service provider. For ads, Google AdMob receives the data described above. We don’t share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Where your data is stored

Your synced progress (Cloud Firestore) is stored on Google servers in the European Union. Sign-in data (Firebase Authentication) and ad data may be processed by Google in other countries, including the United States. These transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account and synced progress: until you delete your account.
  • Security data such as sign-in logs: kept by Google for a limited time under its own retention rules.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you, then deleted.
  • Data saved on your phone: until you uninstall the app.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Live Life Free: Learn ETFs account in two ways:

  • In the app: open your profile, tap Delete account, and confirm it’s you (choose your Google account, or enter your password). Your account is deleted straight away.
  • By email, without the app: send a message to forward-email@sausisinteractive.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your account (email address, name, profile picture link, user ID) and all progress stored online.

What is kept: security logs such as sign-in times may be kept by Google for a limited time under its own retention rules. Progress saved on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you and get a copy,
  • correct data that is wrong,
  • delete your data,
  • restrict how we use it, for example while a correction is being checked,
  • data portability: receive the data you gave us (your account and progress) in a common, machine-readable format, or have it sent to someone else,
  • object to uses based on our legitimate interest,
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for ads: tap the support button, then Ad privacy choices). This does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email forward-email@sausisinteractive.com. We will answer within one month. We may first ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, please contact us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Malta this is the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC): idpc.org.mt. If you live in another EU country, you can also complain to the authority there.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Not financial advice

The app is for learning only. Its simulators use past or made-up data and are not investment advice. See our Terms and Conditions.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
